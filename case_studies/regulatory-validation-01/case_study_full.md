# Integrated case study

The synthetic formulation exercises identity resolution, SVHC evidence, condition-based Annex XVII restrictions, partial harmonised classification evidence, the oral acute-toxicity calculation, SDS consistency review, manual review and versioned provenance. NMP is the sole Candidate List match. NMP, benzene and toluene are Annex XVII potential matches. Formaldehyde supplies the current harmonised oral ATE. No no-match output is interpreted as unregulated, safe or compliant.
